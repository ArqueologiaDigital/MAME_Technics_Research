cpu/sharc: implement the fixed-point multiplier, signed average and TOGGLE ASTAT

The single-function fixed-point multiplier and MAC family, the fixed-point
signed average (ALU op 0x09, the twin of the floating-point FAVG) and TOGGLE
ASTAT were unimplemented: the interpreter threw and the DRC emitted nothing.
Both engines now implement them.

MN is taken from the sign of the delivered value, so the fractional forms read
bit 63 -- the convention the existing fractional multipliers already follow.
