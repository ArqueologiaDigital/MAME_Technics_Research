cpu/sharc: let a variant place its internal memory elsewhere

The 2106x core hardcodes 0x20000 as the internal-memory base in the reset PC,
the interrupt vector base, the DRC's memory accessors and the frontend's loop
checks. Route all of them through virtuals so a variant with a different layout
can exist. The 21060 and 21062 keep their current values, so nothing they do
changes.

m_blocks becomes optional, and the block-geometry assert states that a device
declaring no blocks is exempt rather than being rewritten to pass by accident.
