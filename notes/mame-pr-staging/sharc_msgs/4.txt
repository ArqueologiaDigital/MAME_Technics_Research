cpu/sharc: apply MODE1.ALUSAT in the DRC's fixed-point ALU

Every interpreter fixed-point ALU operation saturates its result when
MODE1.ALUSAT is set; the DRC's native emitters did not, so the two engines
disagreed on the same program. Add the saturation tail to the fixed-point adds,
subtracts, negate, ABS and dual add/subtract.

The bit is read at runtime, as the interpreter reads it. Baking it in at
translation time would need every path that rewrites MODE1 to invalidate the
cache, and the status-stack pop does not.
